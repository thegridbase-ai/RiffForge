Emilyguitar samples (Karoryfer Samples)

          (GitHub license field CC0-1.0 and the LICENSE file) before use.

What is here and how it was made (RiffForge, 2026-10-04):
- Db2.mp3 .. D6.mp3: notes/<pitch>_mf_rr1.wav (velocity layer 81-120, first round robin), recorded direct
  (DI, both pickups, all knobs on ten). Trimmed to 2.5 s with a 0.5 s fade out, +4 dB, mono 44.1 kHz MP3 96 kbps.
- dead-2.mp3, dead-4.mp3: noises/muted2_rr1.wav and noises/muted4_rr1.wav (muted-string hits), trimmed to 0.3 s
  with a 0.1 s fade out, +1 dB, same encoding.

RiffForge plays these through its own amp chain (services/ampChain.ts) when "Amp: New" is selected.
